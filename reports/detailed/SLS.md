**Category: Speculative / Turnaround (Pre-Revenue Clinical Stage)**

SELLAS is a classic Lynch "long shot" — a late-stage clinical company with a binary binary outcome tied to its pivotal Phase 3 REGAL trial. The REGAL trial readout (approaching the 80th event trigger as of Q2 2026) is the defining binary catalyst.

## PART 7: SECTOR-SPECIFIC EDGE (Oncology)
- **GPS mechanism:** WT1 (Wilms Tumor 1) antigen peptide vaccine — unique immunotherapy approach for AML. Unlike chemotherapy, mechanism targets residual disease.
- **REGAL trial design:** Measuring overall survival in second complete remission AML — an area of significant unmet need (no approved maintenance therapy exists).
- **FDA relationship:** The FDA has engaged with the REGAL trial design. The 80th death event trigger is approaching — timeline is NOW.
- **SLS009 CDK9 inhibition:** Broader potential application in AML + pancreatic cancer (RAS-resistant, which is a large unmet need). Phase 2 data expected Q4 2026.

## PART 8: BULL CASE vs BEAR CASE

**Bull Case:**
1. REGAL trial succeeds — GPS shows OS benefit in AML second CR → BLA submitted → stock gaps 150-200%+ on approval probability
2. SLS009 Phase 2 data (Q4 2026) shows strong signals in pancreatic cancer → immediate pipeline re-rating
3. Partnership/acquisition by major oncology player (Pfizer, BMS, Roche) → premium buyout
4. Orphan drug designation provides 7-year exclusivity post-approval → pricing power for small indication

**Bear Case:**
1. REGAL trial fails to show OS benefit at the 80th death analysis → stock drops 60-70% to cash floor
2. SLS009 Phase 2 disappoints → pipeline value collapses entirely
3. Cash burn exceeds expectations due to expanded trial activities → dilutive raise required
4. AML competitive landscape intensifies (venetoclax, gilteritinib) → even an approved GPS competes hard

**Single biggest variable:** REGAL trial outcome. Period. Nothing else matters near-term.
