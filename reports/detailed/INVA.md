**Exchange:** NASDAQ | **Price (16 Aug 2026):** $21.19 | **Currency:** USD

---

## PART 1: LYNCH CLASSIFICATION
**Category: Asset Play / Fast Grower hybrid**

INVA is a Lynch-style "hidden asset" play. The royalty stream from GSK's respiratory blockbusters (BREO ELLIPTA, ANORO ELLIPTA, TRELEGY) is a durable, high-margin annuity. On top of this sits Innoviva Specialty Therapeutics (IST), a growing commercial platform with 46% YoY revenue growth. The overall company is growing total revenue at +19% YoY (Q2 2026). Lynch would classify this as an Asset Play with Fast Grower characteristics — the market is not pricing the royalty asset correctly due to investment mark-to-market noise in reported earnings.

**Classification change:** IST is growing so fast (46% YoY) that it may dominate the P&L within 2 years, converting this from an Asset Play to a pure Fast Grower. This transition is UNDERPRICED.

## PART 2: THE MOAT (Buffett)
**Rating: Narrow — but highly durable within its niche**

- **Royalty moat:** BREO ELLIPTA is protected by patents running into the 2030s. These are contractual royalty rights — no operating risk, no competition risk to INVA directly, just volume risk on underlying GSK product sales. BREO and ANORO are well-established respiratory brands.
- **IST switching costs:** Critical care drugs (GIAPREZA for vasodilatory shock, XACDURO for hospital pneumonia, XERAVA for CIAI) are used in ICU settings. Brand trust and formulary position = sticky.
- **Investment portfolio as moat:** $669.5M in strategic healthcare investments (Armata Pharmaceuticals + others). This creates a "closed-end fund" floor for the stock that is not priced in.

**Moat trend: Stable.** Royalties are contractual and won't grow meaningfully as respiratory drugs are mature. IST is the growth engine — its moat is still being built.

**Single biggest variable:** Armata investment performance. If Armata rerate upward (it's a clinical-stage antimicrobial company), INVA's NAV doubles. If it deteriorates, INVA carries an overhang.
