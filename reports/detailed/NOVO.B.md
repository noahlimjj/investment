## NOTE: SAME COMPANY AS NVO (NYSE ADR)

NOVO.B is the Danish-listed ordinary share of Novo Nordisk. NVO (NYSE) is the American Depositary Receipt representing NOVO.B shares. The underlying business, financials, thesis, and risks are **identical** to the NVO analysis already on file.

The primary difference:
- **NOVO.B** trades in DKK on the Copenhagen Stock Exchange
- **NVO** trades in USD on the NYSE as an ADR (1 ADR ≈ 1 NOVO.B share, adjusted for DKK/USD FX rate)
- DKK 295.90 ≈ USD $43.40 at current FX (DKK/USD ~0.1467), consistent with NVO trading at $45.31

**Currency considerations:**
- Holding NOVO.B means direct DKK exposure (Novo's functional currency)
- Holding NVO means USD-denominated exposure with embedded FX translation
- For a non-Danish investor, NVO removes the DKK settlement friction
- For a Danish/European investor, NOVO.B avoids ADR fees and currency conversion costs
