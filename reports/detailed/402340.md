## PART 1: LYNCH CLASSIFICATION
**Category: Asset Play (leveraged proxy for SK Hynix)**

SK Square is a pure holding company. It owns approximately **20.5% of SK Hynix** (the world's leading HBM memory supplier) plus a portfolio of Korean ICT/digital platform assets. Lynch would classify this as an Asset Play — the market is applying a massive holding-company discount to the value of the underlying assets. The "work" is not in SK Square's own operations — it's in recognising the gap between SK Square's market cap and the fair value of its assets.

**Classification change:** Not changing. This will always be an Asset Play unless SK Square dissolves and becomes an operating company.

---

## PART 2: THE MOAT (Buffett)
**Rating: NARROW — Derived, not intrinsic**

SK Square itself has no direct moat — it is a capital allocator. Its value derives entirely from:
1. **SK Hynix stake (~20.5%):** Wide moat business (see 000660 analysis). This is the primary value driver.
2. **Digital platform investments:** 11st (e-commerce), T map Mobility, ONE store, wavve (streaming). These are mid-tier Korean digital platforms with moderate competitive positions, not dominant moats.

The "holding company moat" argument: SK Square has access to SK Group's capital, relationships, and management talent. This is a narrow advantage — it can co-invest in high-quality assets, but it doesn't independently generate extraordinary returns.

---

## PART 3: MANAGEMENT QUALITY (Buffett)
**Rating: Good — Active value-unlocking strategy**

- **Capital allocation strategy:** "Capital recycling" — divesting non-core digital assets and redeploying into AI and semiconductor infrastructure. This is sound logic.
- **Shareholder returns:** Active share buybacks and buyback-then-cancellation programs. Management is actively working to close the NAV discount.
- **Track record:** H1 2026 cumulative net profit of ₩27.05 trillion (8x vs H1 2025) — largely due to SK Hynix equity method gains. Management has not destroyed value; the SK Hynix stake has been held and appreciated massively.
- **Candor:** Korean chaebol-level disclosure — adequate but not exceptional by Western standards.

**KEY NAV ANALYSIS:**
- SK Hynix market cap at ₩1,667K: ~₩121T
- SK Square's 20.5% stake: **~₩24.8T**
- SK Square's own market cap: **~₩147T**

Wait — this means SK Square appears to trade at a PREMIUM to just its SK Hynix stake alone. This happens because:
1. The current ₩1,165,000 price × shares ≈ ₩147T market cap
2. But SK Hynix market cap calculation: SK Hynix shares outstanding × ₩1,667K = SK Hynix is one of the largest Korean companies. SK Square's 20.5% stake value can be far larger than the holding company's own market cap depending on SK Hynix's total share count.

**Recalculation needed:** SK Hynix shares outstanding ≈ 728 million shares. Market cap = 728M × ₩1,667,000 = ₩1,213 trillion. SK Square 20.5% stake = **₩249 trillion**. vs SK Square market cap of ₩147 trillion = SK Square trades at **41% discount to NAV** from SK Hynix stake alone.

The discount is the opportunity.

## PART 8: BULL CASE vs BEAR CASE

**Bull Case:**
1. SK Hynix re-rates to analyst target of ₩3.16M → SK Square NAV jumps to ~₩500T → stock follows to ₩2.5M+ (+115%)
2. SK Square closes NAV discount via announced buybacks, asset sales, or structural simplification
3. Digital assets (T map Mobility, ONE store) achieve standalone value via IPO or sale
4. Korean government or SK Group takes action to close chaebol holding company discounts (precedent exists with other groups)

**Bear Case:**
1. SK Hynix disappoints on HBM4 (Samsung catches up) → SK Hynix falls 30-40% → SK Square falls proportionally
2. Holding company discount widens rather than closes as governance concerns persist
3. Digital platform assets (11st) deteriorate — 11st has struggled competitively against Coupang
4. Korean macroeconomic weakness compounds discount

**Single biggest variable:** SK Hynix's stock price performance. SK Square is essentially a leveraged, discounted bet on SK Hynix.

The 41% NAV discount is real. This is essentially buying SK Hynix at a 41% discount via SK Square. However, the discount could persist for years (structural feature of Korean chaebols). The digital asset overhang creates additional uncertainty. This is best understood as a paired trade with direct SK Hynix exposure (000660).
