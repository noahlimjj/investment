## 3. Segment Dynamics & Core Drivers
- **Taobao & Tmall Group (TTG):** Generates ~40% of revenue and >75% of operating profit. Facing intense low-price competition from PDD Holdings (Pinduoduo/Temu) and content-driven livestream commerce on Douyin (ByteDance) and Kuaishou. Customer Management Revenue (CMR) growth has decelerated to low single digits (+2% to +4%). Management shifted strategy to defending Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV) and user experience by eliminating merchant take-rate increases.
- **Cloud Intelligence Group:** ~11% of group revenue. Accelerating AI infrastructure revenue (>100% YoY growth in AI-related cloud contracts). However, price cuts in commodity compute and aggressive price competition with Tencent Cloud and Huawei Cloud restrict EBITA margins to ~6–8%.
- **Alibaba International Digital Commerce (AIDC):** AliExpress, Trendyol, Lazada. Revenue growing rapidly (+25% to +35%), but operating at large adjusted EBITA losses due to aggressive subsidy investments to compete with Temu and Shein in Europe, Middle East, and Southeast Asia.
- **Cainiao Smart Logistics:** Deeply integrated into domestic and cross-border delivery. Planned IPO was cancelled to retain end-to-end operational control.

## 4. Capital Allocation & Shareholder Returns
- **Buffett's $1 Retained Earnings Test:** Over FY21–FY26, cumulative retained earnings grew by CNY ~340B while market cap contracted from peak $600B+ to ~$265B. The rolling 5-year test is deeply negative due to geopolitical de-rating and China tech multiple collapse.
- **Buyback Discipline:** Management repurchased over CNY 320B ($45B+ USD) of stock between FY22 and FY26, retiring ~12.5% of outstanding shares (diluted ADS count fell from 2,748M in FY21 to 2,404M in FY26). Buybacks retired shares at average prices of $75–$95, creating tangible per-share accretion.
- **Dividend Introduction:** Alibaba established an annual regular dividend plus special dividends (totaling ~$4.0B–$4.5B annually, ~3.0% yield).

## 5. Structural Governance & Macro Risks
- **VIE Structure Risk:** Foreign investors own shares in a Cayman Islands holding company that holds contractual agreements with domestic PRC entities. Under Chinese law, contracts may not confer genuine equity ownership.
- **Geopolitical & Tariff Frictions:** US-China technological containment (restrictions on advanced semiconductor exports like Nvidia H200/B200 to Alibaba Cloud) and potential US delisting or investment restrictions.
- **Singapore Resident Tax Dynamics:** As a Cayman Islands company, dividends paid to Singapore residents do not incur US dividend withholding tax (0% WHT). No US estate tax situs under 26 USC §2104.
