# Skeptic Audit: ACAD (ACADIA Pharmaceuticals Inc.) — as of 2026-10-13

## 1. Pre-Mortem (Written before reading the Quant model)
**The Structural Downside Thesis (Business worth >50% less in 5 years):**
ACADIA Pharmaceuticals reached profitability on the commercial launch of Daybue. The structural risk is **the plateauing of Daybue patient adoption combined with the terminal expiration of Nuplazid's commercial exclusivity**.

The decline path:
1. **Daybue GI Attrition & Real-World Compliance:** Trofinetide causes severe gastrointestinal adverse effects (diarrhea in >80% of patients). While desperate caregivers initiated therapy rapidly at launch, real-world registry data indicates 12-month patient dropouts of 25%–30%. If compliance fatigue caps US patient penetration at ~1,500–2,000 active patients, Daybue revenue will plateau below $400M.
2. **Exhausted Nuplazid Lifecycle:** Nuplazid failed in multiple pivotal label-expansion trials (dementia-related psychosis, major depressive disorder, negative symptoms of schizophrenia). It remains confined to Parkinson's disease psychosis (~$600M sales) and faces patent loss in 2030.
3. **Pipeline Development Setbacks:** ACADIA's late-stage pipeline relies heavily on acquired assets like ACP-101 (carbetocin for Prader-Willi syndrome), which has historically produced ambiguous clinical trial endpoints across multiple prior sponsors.
4. **Multiple De-rating:** If top-line growth stalls at ~$1.0B–$1.1B while R&D spend escalates, operating margin will compress toward 10%–12%, causing the equity to de-rate toward $8–$10 per share.
