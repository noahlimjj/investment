## 3. Product Portfolio & Commercial Catalysts
- **Nuplazid (pimavanserin):** Approved for hallucinations and delusions associated with Parkinson's disease psychosis (PDP). Stable commercial baseline generating ~$580M–$600M annually with ~90% gross margins. Key patent exclusivity extends through 2030 following favorable patent dispute resolutions.
- **Daybue (trofinetide):** The first and only FDA-approved therapy for Rett syndrome (launched April 2023). Generated ~$350M+ in its first full commercial year. Key clinical headwind is gastrointestinal tolerability (diarrhea in >80% of patients), which leads to real-world patient dropouts. European and Canadian regulatory filings represent next-wave expansion.
- **Pipeline Programs:** ACP-101 (intranasal carbetocin for Prader-Willi syndrome) in Phase 3; early-stage antisense oligonucleotides (ASOs) and selective ion channel modulators for neurodevelopmental disorders.
