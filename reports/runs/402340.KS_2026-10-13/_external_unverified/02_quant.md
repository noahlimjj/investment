## E. Sensitivity & Capital Allocation Constraints
- **The Fair Trade Act 20% Floor:** SK Square owns 146.10M shares of SK Hynix out of 730.49M shares issued, which is exactly **20.0002%**. Under Korea's Monopoly Regulation and Fair Trade Act, a holding company must hold at least 20% of a listed subsidiary.
- **Consequence:** SK Square **cannot monetize or sell a single share of SK Hynix** to fund share repurchases or pay special dividends. Capital allocation is 100% hostage to regular dividend flows and minority tech stake divestitures.
