- **Number audit:** 8 balance sheet and portfolio asset lines audited against DART regulatory filings (제5기 사업보고서 and Q2 2026 portfolio update). Key findings:
  1. *Regulatory Lockup:* Verified that SK Square holds exactly 146,100,000 shares of SK Hynix (20.0002% of issued shares). Under Korea's Fair Trade Act, a holding company must hold ≥20% of a listed subsidiary. The company **cannot sell any Hynix shares** to close the discount.
  2. *Look-Through Premium:* Discovered that at 857,000 KRW, SK Square trades above the total un-discounted intrinsic gross value of its holdings (818,808 KRW).
  3. *Unlisted Portfolio Assets:* Non-hynix assets (11Street, T map Mobility, WAVVE) generate operating losses and represent only 2.6% of gross value even after a 30% haircut.
- **Capital Allocation Grade: C**
  - While management executed modest buybacks and cancelled KRW ~310B of treasury shares, total shareholder return yield is negligible (<0.5%) relative to market cap.
  - Capital allocation is paralyzed by the 20% regulatory floor, leaving management unable to redeploy capital out of memory chips into other investments.
- **Quality of Earnings:** Misleading. Consolidated financial statements incorporate equity-method earnings from SK Hynix that are not distributed as cash dividends.
- **Bear thesis:** Double-whammy contraction. As the memory cycle turns, SK Hynix's operating profit contracts by 60%+, reducing NAV by half. Simultaneously, market liquidity dries up and holding company discounts expand from 47% to 55%+, driving SK Square shares down more than 60%.
- **Primary Downside Catalysts:**
  1. *SK Hynix Margin Peak:* Any price correction in SK Hynix shares will be amplified in SK Square.
  2. *Value-Up Regulatory Disappointment:* Lack of concrete legislative tax incentives for Chaebol parent-company mergers or restructuring.
