# Skeptic Audit: INCY (Incyte Corporation) — as of 2026-10-13

## 1. Pre-Mortem (Written before reading the Quant model)
**The Structural Downside Thesis (Business worth >50% less in 5 years):**
Incyte Corporation is currently basking in the peak earnings power of its flagship JAK inhibitor Jakafi, complemented by Opzelura's commercial ascent. The core structural danger is **the unavoidable arithmetic of the late-2028 Jakafi loss of exclusivity (LOE) cliff**.

The decline path:
1. **The Irreplaceable Anchor:** Jakafi generates ~$3.2B in global revenue and the vast majority of Incyte's operating cash flow. In late 2028, primary US patents expire. Generic filers are already preparing abbreviated new drug applications (ANDAs). Within 18 months of generic entry, branded Jakafi net realized revenue will drop by 60%–70%.
2. **Opzelura Growth Caps:** While Opzelura is an excellent topical formulation, dermatology payers heavily manage topical JAK inhibitors behind step-therapy protocols, and FDA class black-box warnings (MACE, malignancy, thrombosis) restrict broad over-the-counter-style adoption. If Opzelura peaks at $1.5B rather than the hoped-for $2.5B, it leaves an unfillable $1.5B revenue crater.
3. **Pipeline Attrition:** Developing novel oncology and autoimmune blockbusters is inherently risky. If early-stage assets (mutated CALR antibody, CDK2 inhibitors) encounter safety issues or Phase 3 efficacy setbacks, Incyte will face an unmitigated top-line contraction from 2029 to 2032.
4. **Multiple Compression:** Biopharma equities approaching patent cliffs do not trade at 14x P/E; they de-rate to 7x–9x trough earnings.

If Jakafi revenue collapses and pipeline offsets lag, operating profit will fall from $1.5B to ~$600M, dragging equity value down to $40–$50 per share.
