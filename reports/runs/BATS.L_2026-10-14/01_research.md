## 1. Company Profile & Core Operations
British American Tobacco p.l.c. (BAT) is one of the world's leading multi-category consumer goods companies, operating across 175+ markets.
The business is organized into three primary product categories:
1. **Combustibles (Cigarettes):**
   - Core global drive brands: *Dunhill, Kent, Lucky Strike, Pall Mall, Rothmans*, and US power brands *Newport, Camel, Natural American Spirit* (via Reynolds American acquisition).
   - Combustibles generate ~81% of group net revenues and ~88% of group operating profit.
   - Volume trends: Global cigarette volume declines at -5% to -8% annually, but price realization (+6% to +9%) has historically protected and expanded gross profit.
2. **New Categories (Smokeless & Reduced-Risk Products):**
   - **Vapour (Vuse):** Leading global closed-system vapour brand (value share >35% globally, #1 in key markets, though pressured in the US by illicit disposable vapes from China).
   - **Heated Products (glo):** Tobacco heating devices (glo Hyper Pro) competing against PMI's IQOS in Japan, Italy, and Eastern Europe.
   - **Modern Oral Nicotine (Velo):** Rapidly growing nicotine pouch portfolio, #1 in Europe and expanding in the US and emerging markets.
   - Milestone: New Categories reached profitability ahead of schedule (FY2023/FY2024 breakeven achieved), generating £3.4B+ in revenue.
3. **Traditional Oral:**
   - US moist snuff (*Grizzly, Kodiak*), generating steady, high-margin cash flow.

## 5. Regulatory & Sector Landscape (The Load-Bearing Variables)
1. **US Illicit Disposable Vapes:** Proliferation of unregulated, flavored disposable e-cigarettes from China (e.g. Geek Bar, Elf Bar) has captured ~50% of the US vape market. FDA and US Customs stepped up import alerts and joint federal task forces in 2025/2026; enforcement actions directly benefit BAT's compliant Vuse brand.
2. **US Menthol Ban:** The FDA proposed a ban on menthol cigarettes (Newport is ~50% of Reynolds' US profit). However, implementation has faced multiple White House delays and will face years of federal court litigation under the APA. Even if enacted, international experience (EU, Canada) shows 80%+ volume retention through non-menthol conversion and menthol alternatives.
3. **Modern Oral (Nicotine Pouches):** Velo is growing at triple-digit rates in the US and Europe, capturing profitable smokeless nicotine market share with high consumer retention and zero combustible stigma.
