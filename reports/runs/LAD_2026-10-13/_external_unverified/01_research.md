## 3. Core Business Drivers & Dealership Economics
- **Consolidation Scale:** Lithia is the largest automotive retailer in the United States and expanded into the UK via the Pendragon acquisition. Network scale allows procurement efficiencies in financing, insurance (F&I), and fleet parts.
- **GPU Normalization:** Gross Profit per Unit (GPU) has completed its post-COVID mean-reversion, falling from peak levels of $5,500+ down to mid-cycle normalized levels of ~$2,100–$2,400. Operating margins have settled at 4.2%, matching long-term historical norms.
- **Parts & Service (Fixed Operations) Moat:** Fixed operations (parts, maintenance, collision repair) generate >45% of total gross profit with high margins (50%+ gross margin), providing recession-resilient recurring cash flow during vehicle sales downturns.

## 4. Capital Allocation & Cannibal Share Buybacks
- **Buffett's $1 Retained Earnings Test:** Retained earnings expanded by ~$3.2B over FY20–FY25 while market cap expanded by ~$3.8B, generating $1.19 of market value per $1 retained.
- **Aggressive Cannibal Share Retirement:** Management has executed aggressive counter-cyclical share repurchases, retiring >24% of shares outstanding since 2021 (diluted share count fell from 29.0M to 21.98M). In 2025 alone, LAD repurchased $960.9M of stock at average prices well below intrinsic value.
