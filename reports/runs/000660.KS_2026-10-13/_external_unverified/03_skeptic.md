# Skeptic Audit: 000660.KS (SK Hynix Inc.) — as of 2026-10-13

## 1. Pre-Mortem (Written before reading the Quant model)
**The Structural Downside Thesis (Business worth >50% less in 5 years):**
SK Hynix is currently priced at the absolute euphoric pinnacle of a generational memory cycle. The narrative posits that High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) has permanently decoupled memory chips from historical commoditization cycles into proprietary AI hardware. This thesis will prove false, exactly as past "memory supercycle" narratives failed in 2018 and 2021.

The structural mechanism for a 50%+ equity collapse is twofold:
1. **Supply Side Flood:** Samsung Electronics (with a cash pile exceeding KRW 100T) and Micron Technology are aggressively qualifying 12-layer and 16-layer HBM3E and HBM4 with Nvidia, AMD, and custom ASIC vendors. As Samsung's massive capacity ramps through 2027, the extreme HBM supply deficit transforms into a glut. HBM pricing collapses by 40%–60%, compressing gross margins back toward historical commodity DRAM levels.
2. **AI Capex Digestion:** Hyperscaler cloud capex faces a severe return-on-investment reckoning. If inference monetization slows, GPU cluster buildouts will be delayed or downsized. In memory downturns, pricing does not decline linearly—it falls below cash costs.
3. **Crushing Fixed Depreciation:** In response to peak demand, SK Hynix committed tens of trillions of won to new fab construction (M15X in Cheongju, the Yongin Semiconductor Cluster, and the Indiana packaging facility). When demand normalizes, these multi-trillion won capex investments convert into inescapable depreciation charges against collapsing revenues.
4. **Historical Precedent:** In 2023, SK Hynix swung from record profits to a staggering **KRW 7.73T operating loss** with negative free cash flow of KRW -12.5T. A return to mid-cycle profitability of ~25% operating margins on normalized sales cuts earnings by more than two-thirds.

## 3. Quality of Earnings & Cyclical Reality Check
- **Earnings Quality is Cyclically Flattered:** Q2-26 operating margin of 76.3% is an anomaly in semiconductor history. Memory margins have mean-reverted across every single cycle over the past 40 years.
- **Operating Cash Flow vs Capex:** In H1 2026, SK Hynix generated huge OCF (KRW ~55T), but annual capex commitments are scaling to KRW 55T–65T annually.
- **Lynch Inventory Check:** H1-26 inventory stood at KRW 15.8T. While inventory-to-sales ratios look healthy during peak shipment quarters, customer inventory build at hyperscalers creates phantom demand that evaporates overnight when order books trim.
