# Skeptic audit: 000660.KS (as of 2026-10-13)

## 1. Pre-mortem (written before reading the model)
Written 2026-10-14 from `01_research.md` and `facts.json` only, before opening `02_quant.md` or `02_model.py`.

**Most likely structural reason SK hynix is worth materially less in 5 years: the supply response mean-reverts peak margins, and HBM's premium gets competed away at the same moment.**
- The P&L is at a margin no memory maker has ever held through a cycle. Q2-26 gross margin was 83.2% and operating margin 76.3% (facts.json Q2_2026). FY2021-FY2025 operating margins were 28.9 / 15.7 / -23.6 / 35.5 / 48.6%, a simple average of 21.0% (my arithmetic). TTM operating profit is 128.7T against a 5-year sum of 82.4T.
- The supply response is already funded and dated. SK hynix's own 2026 capex is guided to the "high KRW 40T range" [WF]. Y2 (35.2T, to 2031) and M17 (19.1T, to 2031) were approved in August 2026. The Yongin phase-1 cleanroom opens in early 2027. Samsung started HBM4 supply in Feb 2026, before SK hynix's Q2 HBM4 mass shipments. CXMT raised CNY 57.9B in July 2026 [SNIPPET]. Management's own words are "we do not believe ... will lead to oversupply **right away**."
- The pricing power is borrowed from customer scarcity, not owned. 2027 HBM pricing is "under discussion", and the CFO dodged on direction. The buyers are concentrated: two customers each about 13% of H1-26 revenue, and the U.S. entity 64%. Once Samsung and Micron qualify at scale, the buyer (NVIDIA et al.) sets HBM price, not SK hynix.
- Why it bites hard: at 1,051.8T the market cap is 8.2x TTM operating profit but 22.3x FY2025 operating profit. If 2029-31 operating margins revert to roughly 25-35% on a revenue base that also deflates with ASPs (Q2 DRAM ASP rose mid-30% QoQ and NAND mid-50% in one quarter, so the revenue line is mostly price, not bits), sustainable owner earnings could land at a fraction of today's run-rate. A 2023-style year (operating margin -24%) is the base rate for a memory downturn, not a tail.
- Secondary pre-mortem risks: (a) capital allocation at the peak. 17.79M new shares were sold via ADS at about KRW 2.24M, then a KRW 40T buyback was authorised 6 weeks later at up to about 1.66M, and capex commitments of 61T+ are being locked in at peak margins. (b) KRW appreciation of about 13% since July compresses KRW revenue on USD-priced chips.
- What the tape already says: the price is -50.6% from the 52w high and fell -21.6% in six sessions to 10-13, while consensus still extrapolates (FY27 EPS +32%). The tape is already pricing this pre-mortem in part. The model has to show how much.
