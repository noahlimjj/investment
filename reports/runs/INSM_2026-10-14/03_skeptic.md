## 1. Blind Pre-Mortem (Written Before Reading Quant Model)
**The Single Most Likely Structural Cause of a >50% Permanent Impairment in 5 Years:**

> **Commercial Payor Step-Therapy & Formulary Gatekeeping Caps Brensocatib Peak Sales Under $2.5B While Fixed SG&A and Pipeline Burn Exceed $1.0B Annually.**
> 
> Non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis (NCFB) is currently managed with low-cost standard of care: generic hypertonic saline, airway clearance vests/PEP devices, and off-label macrolides (azithromycin). While the ASPEN Phase 3 trial demonstrated a 20% reduction in annualized exacerbation rates, US commercial payors and Medicare Part D formularies will aggressively implement prior authorization (PA) barriers, requiring patients to fail multiple inexpensive clearance modalities before approving brensocatib at orphan/specialty pricing ($150,000–$250,000/year). 
> 
> If real-world compliance falters or payors restrict access strictly to frequent exacerbators (≥3 exacerbations/year), brensocatib peak US sales will plateau at $1.5B–$2.0B rather than the $10B+ implied by today's $21B market cap. Meanwhile, ongoing commercial launch expenses and exploratory clinical trials will burn >$800M annually, forcing further dilutive equity offerings that permanently impair per-share value.
