## 3. Product Architecture & Neuroscience Pipeline
- **1. Ulixacaltamide (PRAX-944):**
  - Potent, highly selective, orally bioavailable small-molecule T-type calcium channel blocker (Cav3.1, Cav3.2, Cav3.3).
  - Indication: Essential tremor (ET). Essential tremor affects ~7 million individuals in the US, with no new FDA-approved pharmacotherapy in over 50 years.
  - Clinical Status: Following positive Phase 2b Essential1 results, the Phase 3 Essential3 program is actively enrolling, with registrational readouts expected in 2026/2027.
- **2. PRAX-628:**
  - Functionally selective small-molecule voltage-gated sodium channel (Nav) blocker designed to target hyperexcitable neuronal states while sparing normal CNS firing.
  - Indication: Focal onset seizures (epilepsy).
  - Clinical Status: Phase 2a photo-paroxysmal response (PPR) proof-of-concept trial demonstrated 100% complete response at target doses with favorable tolerability. Phase 2b trial in focal epilepsy underway.
- **3. Relutrigine (PRAX-562):**
  - Nav blocker in development for SCN2A and SCN8A developmental and epileptic encephalopathies (DEEs).
- **Patent Runway:** Composition of matter patents for ulixacaltamide and PRAX-628 extend to ~2039–2040. Terminal value post-2040 is set to **0.0%**.
