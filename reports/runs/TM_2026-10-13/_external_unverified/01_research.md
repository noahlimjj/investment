## 3. Core Business Drivers & Segment Dynamics
- **Hybrid Hegemony:** Toyota's long-term bet on Hybrid Electric Vehicles (HEVs) rather than pure Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) paid off extraordinarily well in 2023–2024. Hybrids now account for >38% of global Toyota/Lexus volume, yielding premium margins without BEV price discounting.
- **Certification Testing Scandals:** Domestic Japanese vehicle certification issues (Daihatsu, Toyota Industries, and Toyota Motor) resulted in temporary production pauses in late 2024 and early 2025, dinging domestic consumer sentiment and triggering governance scrutiny.
- **FX Sensitivity:** Weak yen (150–160 JPY/USD) provided an enormous earnings windfall in FY24–FY25 (every 1 JPY appreciation vs USD cuts annual operating profit by ~JPY 50B). Any BOJ rate hikes and yen strengthening normalize operating profits.

## 4. Capital Allocation & Shareholder Returns
- **Buffett's $1 Retained Earnings Test:** Over FY20–FY25, market cap rose by ~JPY 16T while retaining ~JPY 12T. Created ~$1.33 per $1 retained.
- **Dividend & Buybacks:** Consistent progressive dividend policy (dividend yield ~3.1%) augmented by regular share repurchases of JPY 500B–1,000B annually.
- **Cross-Shareholding Unwinding:** Under Tokyo Stock Exchange corporate governance pressure, Toyota has begun orderly divestitures of historic cross-shareholdings (Denso, Aisin, telecom holdings), redeploying capital into share buybacks and battery R&D.
