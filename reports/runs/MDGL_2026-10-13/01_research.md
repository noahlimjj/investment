## 3. Product Architecture & Competitive Moat
- **Rezdiffra (resmetirom):** Once-daily oral thyroid hormone receptor (THR)-beta selective agonist approved on March 14, 2024, for noncirrhotic nonalcoholic steatohepatitis (MASH) with moderate to advanced liver fibrosis (F2 to F3).
- **First-in-Class Advantage:** As the first approved MASH drug, Rezdiffra secured preferred tier placement on commercial formularies (UnitedHealthcare, CVS Caremark, Cigna) without requiring invasive liver biopsy for reimbursement.
- **The GLP-1 Overhang:** Incretin therapies (tirzepatide, semaglutide) demonstrate potent MASH resolution, though resmetirom uniquely demonstrates direct hepatic fibrosis improvement without muscle wasting. Combination therapy (resmetirom + GLP-1) represents the emerging clinical paradigm.
- **European Regulatory Timeline:** European Medicines Agency (EMA) review underway; European commercial launch targeted for 2026.
