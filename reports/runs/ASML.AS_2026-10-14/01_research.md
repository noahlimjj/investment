## 3. Business Facts & Peter Lynch Two-Minute Drill
- **What ASML Does:** Monopoly designer and manufacturer of Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) photolithography machines (0.33 NA and next-gen 0.55 High-NA) and dominant provider of Deep Ultraviolet (DUV) immersion lithography systems. EUV is physically mandatory to print semiconductor features below 7nm/5nm/3nm/2nm.
- **Customers:** TSMC (~40-45% of system sales), Samsung Electronics (~25%), Intel (~15%), Micron, SK Hynix.
- **Installed Base Service Revenue:** High-margin recurring service and upgrade contracts account for ~22% of total revenue (€7.2B in FY25), providing a growing, high-margin cash annuity.
- **Next-Gen Catalyst:** Commercial ramp of High-NA EUV (EXE:5000/5200) priced at €350M–€400M per tool for A16/A14 logic nodes and advanced 3D DRAM nodes.
