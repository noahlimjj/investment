# Value Skeptic Report: ASML.AS (ASML Holding N.V.)
As of: 2026-10-14 · Official Raw Close: €1,575.80 · Spot Price: €1,509.60

## 1. Independent Blind Pre-Mortem (Structural Downside Risks)
*Written independently before evaluating scenario weights:*
- **The China Demand Cliff:** In FY23–FY24, China surged to 39–49% of ASML's total quarterly tool shipments as Chinese domestic fabs (SMIC, CXMT) pre-bought immersion DUV tools ahead of anticipated sanctions. The Dutch government, under US trilateral pressure, has expanded licensing controls to cover advanced immersion DUV (NXT:1970i/1980i) and revoked maintenance/servicing licenses. As Chinese pre-ordering subsides, ASML faces a multi-billion euro revenue hole in China that Western foundries cannot easily absorb.
- **The High-NA Cost & Monopsony Bottleneck:** High-NA EUV tools carry an exorbitant price tag of €350M–€400M each. TSMC, which generates over 40% of ASML's revenue, publicly stated that High-NA EUV is too expensive for initial 2nm/A16 production and is stretching standard 0.33 NA EUV with multi-patterning instead. With Intel slashing capex and delaying major fab buildouts, ASML faces monopsony pricing pressure and push-outs of its highest-margin roadmap.
- **Valuation Detachment from Gravity:** At €1,509.60, the market capitalization sits at €580B ($650B USD) with an Owner Earnings yield of only 1.55%. The reverse DCF proves the tape is discounting a constant 22.3% revenue CAGR for a decade—meaning ASML's revenue must reach €244B/year. Any pause in AI accelerator capex will trigger a brutal de-rate.
