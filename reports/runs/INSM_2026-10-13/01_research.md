## 3. Product Architecture & Pipeline Assets
- **1. Arikayce (liposomal amikacin inhalation suspension):**
  - Commercialized for Mycobacterium avium complex (MAC) lung disease in patients with limited treatment options.
  - FY25 sales reached ~$450M+, with ongoing Phase 3 ARISE and ENCORE trials testing frontline MAC therapy to expand the addressable label.
  - Exclusivity extends through 2033 (orphan drug exclusivity + formulation patents).
- **2. Brensocatib (DPP1 / Cathepsin C inhibitor):**
  - First-in-class oral dipeptidyl peptidase 1 (DPP1) inhibitor designed to shut down neutrophil serine proteases (NSPs).
  - Landmark Phase 3 ASPEN trial met its primary endpoint with high statistical significance in non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis (NCFB), reducing annualized rate of pulmonary exacerbations (p=0.0019 for 10mg, p=0.0002 for 25mg).
  - NCFB is a massive untreated disease affecting >450,000 diagnosed patients in the US alone with zero FDA-approved pharmacotherapies.
  - Filing completed; commercial launch anticipated 2025/2026. Brensocatib composition of matter patents extend to ~2039–2040.
  - Pipeline expansions: Phase 2 trials in chronic rhinosinusitis without nasal polyps (CRSwNP) and hidradenitis suppurativa (HS).
- **3. TPIP (Treprostinil Palmitil Inhalation Powder / INS1009):**
  - Inhaled prostanoid prodrug in Phase 2 for pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) and pulmonary hypertension with interstitial lung disease (PH-ILD), competing against United Therapeutics (Tyvaso DPI).
